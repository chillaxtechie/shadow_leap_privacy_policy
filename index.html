Game progression (high scores, sparks, unlocked skins) is saved strictly on your local device. We use Google AdMob to serve in-game rewarded and interstitial advertisements.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Chronicle Play ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information in connection with the mobile application Shadow Leap: Slipstream ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification details (e.g., name, phone number, physical address).
  • Account credentials or passwords.
  • Biometric data, contacts, camera feeds, or microphones.
  • Precise GPS or background geolocation data.
  • Financial or payment card details.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

To support free-to-play functionality, this App integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK provided by Google LLC). Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and device identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address (used to infer general country/city level location for regional ad delivery), and unique device identifiers.
  • Advertising & Interaction Data: Views, clicks, video completions for Rewarded Ads (such as revives or 2x score boosters), and interstitial ad interactions.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: App crash logs, ANR reports, device model, operating system version, and system performance metrics.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising

4. Local Device Data Storage

All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device via standard HTML5 Local Storage and Android App Sandboxed Storage:

  • High scores, personal best distance records, and ghost trajectory recordings.
  • Accumulated in-game sparks currency.
  • Unlocked runner skins (Night Runner Shadow, Emerald Phantom, Blade Phantom, Neon Ronin) and trail colors.
  • User preferences (audio mute toggle, controls preference: buttons vs. touch gestures).

This data remains entirely on your device and is not synced to external servers. Clearing your application cache or uninstalling the App will erase local progress.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Shadow Leap: Slipstream is designed for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

7. User Rights & Opt-Out Controls (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full control over advertising identifiers on your Android device:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On your Android device, go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads) to reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your Android settings. When opted out, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized based on your activity.
  • Right to Erasure: Because all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all records at any time by going to Settings > Apps > Shadow Leap > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.

8. Security Measures

We maintain strict software integrity standards. All web assets are bundled directly within the offline APK and loaded through secure, sandboxed Android WebView architectures (androidx.webkit.WebViewAssetLoader). No external unverified third-party scripts are loaded into the game environment.
